2. Goals and Objectives:

Game Overview:
LingoBingo is a vocabulary game designed for all ages, with easy, medium, and hard levels to help people learn words in a fun and compelling way. The main components are word cards, bingo cards, and colored tiles (yellow, red, and green).

- Word Cards: Each word card has two sides. The front displays the definition and an image representing the word. The back shows the definition and three synonyms: an easy synonym (grades 1 – 5), the word itself (medium), and a hard synonym (grades 9 – 12).
- Bingo Card: The bingo card features a 4x4 grid of randomly chosen words from the set provided.

Gameplay: 
The gamemaster (the app) displays a word card face-up (definition and image). Players check their bingo card and, if they think they have the word, place a yellow tile on it. Afterward, the word card is flipped to reveal the answer. If correct, the yellow tile is replaced with a green tile; if incorrect, it’s replaced with a red tile. The game continues with new word cards until the player achieves a bingo (a complete row, column, or diagonal). The game then ends, and the player can start a new round.

Objectives:
- Improve vocabulary skills for users of all ages and skill levels.
- Make learning new words engaging and enjoyable through interactive gameplay.
- Provide different difficulty levels (easy, medium, hard) to cater to a wide range of learners.
- Support visual and contextual learning with images and definitions.
- Encourage repeated play and self-improvement.
- Focus on a single-player experience for the initial mobile app release.

Success Metrics:

- Daily Active Users (DAU)
- Retention Rate
- User Ratings & Reviews


3. Gameplay and Core Mechanics:

Note: In the current implementation, "placing a tile" refers to the player simply clicking on a word cell and its color changing to indicate correctness (yellow for selection, green for correct, red for incorrect). Tile animations may be added in the future.

User Flow:
1. User opens the LingoBingo app.
2. User selects a difficulty level (easy, medium, hard).
3. User is presented with a new bingo card (4x4 grid of words).
4. The app displays a word card (definition and image) at the top of the screen.
5. User places a yellow tile on the word in their bingo grid that they believe matches the definition.
6. User taps the “Done” button.
7. The word card is flipped to reveal the answer.
8. Tiles are updated to green (correct) or red (incorrect).
9. Steps 4–8 repeat until the user achieves a bingo (row, column, or diagonal).
10. Bingo notification/animation is shown.
11. User can start a new game or return to the main menu.

Tile Marking and Feedback Logic:
When a word card is shown, the following scenarios can occur:
1. Word IS on the bingo card, player places a tile on the correct word:
   - The player places a tile on the correct word (matches the definition on the word card) on their bingo card.
   - The tile turns green (correct).

2. Word IS on the bingo card, player places a tile on the wrong word:
   - The player places a tile on a word, but it does not match the definition on the word card.
   - The tile turns red (incorrect).

3. Word IS on the bingo card, player does not place a tile on the correct word:
   - The player does not place a tile on the correct word that matches the definition on the word card (misses it).
   - The tile for the correct word turns red (missed).

4. Word IS NOT on the bingo card, player places a tile on any word:
   - The player places a tile on a word, but the correct word (matches the definition on the word card) is not present on the bingo card.
   - The tile that the player placed turns red (incorrect).

5. Word IS NOT on the bingo card, player does not place a tile:
   - The player does not place a tile on any word.
   - This is correct; nothing changes, and the game continues.

Controls and Input:
- Tap: Place a tile on a word and navigate menus.
- Simple, touch-based controls optimized for mobile devices.

Screens:
- Home Screen: Game title, start button, difficulty selection.
- Game Screen: Displays the bingo card grid and current word card, plus a 'done' button for players after they place a tile in their bingo grid. Players place a tile on the word that they believe matches the definition on the current word card. After the answer is revealed, tiles are updated to green (correct) or red (incorrect).
- Bingo Notification: Popup or animation when bingo is achieved.

UI/UX Details:
- Tile colors: Yellow (#FBDC6A) for initial selection, Green (#7ED957) for correct, Red (#FF3131) for incorrect.
- Background: White (#FFFFFF).
- Font: Use 'Roboto' or system default, minimum size 15px for words.
- Buttons: Rounded corners, primary color for background is Yellow (#FBDC6A) with Black text (#000000).
- All elements centered horizontally.

Layout: 
- Word card at top, bingo grid centered, “Done” button below grid.
- All game elements (word card, bingo grid, buttons, etc.) must fit on a single screen without requiring any vertical or horizontal scrolling, on all supported device sizes.
- Elements should resize, reposition, or scale as needed to ensure everything is visible at once.
- If the screen is too small, reduce padding, font size, or element size, but never require scrolling.

Animations and Transitions:
- Tiles change color instantly (no animation) in MVP.
- Word card flips with a 0.5s animation when revealing the answer.
- Bingo notification slides in from the top.
- In the future, add tile placement animation and sound effects.

Difficulty Progression:
- Difficulty is chosen at the start (easy, medium, hard).
- Harder difficulties use more challenging synonyms of the word.

Game Progression:
- Endless play: Each game is a new bingo card with randomly selected words.
- No traditional levels, but difficulty is set at the start (easy, medium, hard).
- Players can play as many rounds as they like.

Losing States:
- No traditional “lose” state; the game is about learning and improvement.
- Players can see which words they missed (red tiles) and try again in a new round.

Game Logic:
- Bingo card generation: Randomly fill a 4x4 grid with words from a word list, based on selected difficulty.
- Win detection: Check for completed rows, columns, or diagonals.
- Word data: Each word has a definition, synonyms, and optional image.
- Data source: Use OpenAI API for definitions/synonyms, images generated and/or saved from online.

Game Rules and Edge Cases:
- Only one tile can be placed per word card displayed.
- Player CANNOT change their answer after they place a tile (the cell should be unclickable) before pressing “Done.”
- No time limit per round.
- If user leaves or restarts, current game is lost and a new game starts.
- After bingo, show a summary screen with score and option to play again.
- If player tries to place a tile after “Done,” ignore input.

11. Glossary:
- Bingo Card: The 4x4 grid containing randomly selected vocabulary words for the current game.
- Word Tile: An individual cell on the bingo card, displaying a vocabulary word.
- Word Card: The card displayed at the top of the screen, showing the definition (and image, if available) of the word to be matched.
- Tile: The visual indicator (yellow, green, or red) placed on a word tile to show selection, correctness, or incorrectness.
- Placing a Tile: The act of selecting a word tile, which changes its color to yellow (selected), green (correct), or red (incorrect).
- Yellow Tile: Indicates the player’s selection before the answer is revealed.
- Green Tile: Indicates a correct match after the answer is revealed.
- Red Tile: Indicates an incorrect match or a missed correct word after the answer is revealed.
- Game Phase: The current stage of the game (e.g., selection, checking, bingo achieved).
- Difficulty Level: The chosen challenge level (easy, medium, hard), which determines the synonym shown and the word list used.
- Bingo: Achieving a complete row, column, or diagonal of correct tiles on the bingo card.
- Done Button: The button the player presses to submit their selection(s) for the current word card.
- Bingo Notification: The popup or animation shown when the player achieves a bingo.
- Score: The player’s accumulated points, if scoring is implemented.
- Synonym: An alternative word shown on the answer side of the word card, corresponding to the selected difficulty.
- Image Placeholder: The default icon (e.g., 📚) shown when no image is available for a word.
